About Us

Welcome to Bellis Blossom! We are a passionate team of florists dedicated to creating beautiful arrangements for your special occasions.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, Bellis Blossom is a floral design studio specializing in customized wedding arrangements. We believe that every wedding is unique, and the flowers should reflect each couple's personality, style, and love story.

From beautiful bridal bouquets to elegant ceremony decorations, we are dedicated to bringing your vision to life through thoughtful and personalized floral designs. Whether you're planning an intimate indoor wedding or a romantic outdoor celebration, we work to create arrangements that complement your special day.

At Bellis Blossom, our mission is simple: to turn your floral dreams into reality and make your wedding day even more memorable.
